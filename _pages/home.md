I am a research associate and PhD candidate at the [Chair for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence](https://hcai.eu) at the University of Augsburg, currently supervised by Prof. Dr. Elisabeth André.
I am interested in using explainability methods for human behaviour analysis and feedback generation, for example in motor learning applications.
My goal is to understand human movement in order to provide computer-aided feedback.

I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Augsburg, where I worked on human pose estimation under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Rainer Lienhart.
